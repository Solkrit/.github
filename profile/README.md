## ⚡ About Us

**Solkrit** (formerly known as *SolderLab*) is an electronics product development studio that takes hardware ideas from concept to deployable product. 

Most hardware projects fail not because the idea is wrong, but because they get split across too many hands — a firmware freelancer here, a PCB house there, an enclosure vendor somewhere else, with no one owning the whole system. Solkrit exists to close that gap. We design the circuit, write the firmware, build the enclosure, assemble the prototype, and coordinate manufacturing — as one connected process, not four disconnected handoffs.

If you have a product that needs to go from idea to something you can hold, test, and ship — that's the work we do.

---

## 🛠️ Core Capabilities & Technical Scope

Our work spans the entire hardware and firmware stack:
* **Embedded Systems:** High-reliability firmware on **ESP32**, **STM32**, and **RP2040** platforms.
* **Industrial Connectivity:** Robust protocols including **RS-485 Modbus**, **LTE**, and **MQTT**.
* **PCB Design & Assembly:** Custom multi-layer circuit design, schematic capture, DFM review, and component sourcing.
* **Enclosure Engineering:** 3D-printed and injection-ready housings designed for rugged real-world environments.
* **Full Device Assembly:** Complete end-to-end prototyping and small-batch manufacturing coordination.

*Recent work includes a three-tier industrial IoT gateway with LTE failover and cloud telemetry, and a health-monitoring wearable built for real-world ruggedness and battery efficiency.*

## 👨‍💻 Meet the Founder

**Dhanesh Baheti**, Founder of Solkrit (SolderLab), has been building, breaking, and rebuilding electronics since middle school, long before it became his profession.

* **MyPerro:** Leads product development, designing the hardware architecture for a smart wearable dog collar, managing everything from battery system design to waterproof assembly.
* **Industrial IoT Engineering:** Worked as a contract IIoT engineer for manufacturing clients, building connected monitoring systems that combine hardware, firmware, connectivity, and cloud integration into a cohesive system.
* **Recognition:** Honored at **IIT Bombay Techfest**, the **Atal Innovation Mission**, and **INSPIRE Awards** for applied engineering work. 

Every project at Solkrit runs through this same philosophy: fewer handoffs, greater systemic ownership, and a builder's attention to whether something actually works in the field, not just on paper.
